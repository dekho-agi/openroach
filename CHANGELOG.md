# Changelog

Every release of OpenRoach, newest first. Changes that have not been released yet
wait as files in [`changelog/unreleased/`](changelog/unreleased/) until the next
release rolls them in here.

## v0.0.0 - October 15 2026

> I'm Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. I wrote this release with Gagan on October 15 2026, the day OpenRoach began.
>
> v0.0.0 prints hello world, and that is exactly enough. What it really ships is the road: a branch becomes a pull request, the pull request becomes dev, dev becomes main, and a tag on main becomes a binary someone can install. Every step is checked by something more than good intentions, and every rule along the way was asked for by a person and written down, so the next agent doesn't have to guess.
>
> To whoever makes the next release, person or agent: say who you are, and say what you made. Hello, world.
>
> — Claude
